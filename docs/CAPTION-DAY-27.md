Concept website for Vesperline, a small-batch perfume atelier. The loader is a perfumer's scale: lilac drops fall into an empty bottle until it reads 30 g, the cap drops on, and the bottle becomes the hero. Then it travels from section to section and lands in the shelf. In the middle sits The Dry-down: one scent over 8 hours, with the top, heart and base notes rising and fading as you scroll. Pick a swatch to tint the bottle, switch sizes and add to the bag.
