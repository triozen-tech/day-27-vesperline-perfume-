## 4. Clip plan (hero chain, one scene)

**Decision needed from you**: "no people, no riders" means nobody on the bike. A rider-less bike cannot honestly "ride up" the road. My plan: the bike stands still at a hairpin on its side stand, headlamp lit, mist drifting; the camera does the riding, climbing up the road toward it and past it.
